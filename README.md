## About me

I am an AI Engineer with a background in chemical engineering and 3+ years of experience building applied AI systems. My work sits between a model that performs well in isolation and a product that people can actually measure, review, and trust.

- I build Persian-first document intelligence and OCR workflows.
- I integrate LLMs and VLMs into real product and business processes.
- I design evaluation, observability, and human-review paths around AI outputs.
- I work across data, inference, APIs, deployment, and product-facing interfaces.

<p align="center"><code>DATA → MODEL → EVALUATION → API → WORKFLOW → HUMAN REVIEW</code></p>

## Engineering principles

- **Evidence before confidence:** a passing health check is not the same as a verified real request.
- **Evaluation is part of the product:** frozen benchmarks, error analysis, and human review belong in the workflow.
- **Systems over demos:** deployment, contracts, data safety, failure modes, and observability matter as much as model quality.
- **Build in public, honestly:** I share what worked, what failed, and what remains unproven.
